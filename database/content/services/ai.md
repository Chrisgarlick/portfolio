Most businesses describe the problem as a technology gap. It almost never is. It's a process gap. The model is rarely the bottleneck; the hand-off either side of it is, and that is where the hours go.

## What I build

- **Workflow automation** that takes a repetitive job off your team's plate
- **Data extraction** from PDFs, emails, forms and statements into the systems you already use
- **AI assistants and agents**, with a person in the loop where it matters
- **AI features** inside a web application, built on [Laravel](/services/laravel)

## How it works

We pick one process and map it properly. I build it into your existing systems, test it against your real data, and add the logging and review steps that let you trust it. You get the build, the prompts and the documentation. If it pays for itself, we look at the next one.

## A good first process

The best place to start is a job your team does every week, by hand, in roughly the same way. Typing details from documents into another system is a common one. So is sorting incoming emails, or drafting the same kind of reply again and again.

A good first process happens often, has similar inputs each time, and gives a result a person can check quickly. If it ticks those boxes, there is usually something worth building.

## How I build it

I work with Anthropic's Claude. Structured outputs mean results land in a fixed shape your systems can read. Prompts are versioned like code, so a change can be tracked and undone. Every run is logged. Where data residency matters, I use UK and EU regions.

Where a mistake would be costly, a person reviews the result before it goes anywhere. The AI drafts and proposes. Someone on your team decides.

## Who it suits

It suits a business with a clear, repetitive job and someone who knows that job well. That person matters. They know where the edge cases are, and they can judge whether the output is right.

It doesn't suit a vague brief to add AI somewhere. I'd rather start with the process than the tool.

## Useful, not hyped

I use AI every day, and I'm still not going to sell it to you. It's worth adding where the numbers work and nowhere else. If a simpler automation or a better form would do the job, that's what I will recommend. The [articles](/article) go into the detail.

## Common questions

### Do I need my data in order first?

Not completely. We test against your real documents, so we find out early what works and what doesn't. If something needs tidying first, I'll tell you before the build, not after.

### Will it replace people on my team?

That isn't the aim. The aim is to take a repetitive job off their plate, so they spend more time on work that needs a person. Where it matters, a person stays in the loop and checks the result.

### What if the AI gets something wrong?

It will, sometimes. That's why logging and review steps are part of the build. You can see every input and output, and the riskier steps go to a person first.

### Can it work with the software we already use?

Usually, yes. I build into your existing systems rather than alongside them. That includes tools like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Xero, HubSpot and Notion, through direct API work.
