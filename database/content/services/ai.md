Most businesses describe the problem as a technology gap. It is almost always a process gap. The bottleneck is rarely the model; it is the handoff either side of it, and that is where the hours go.

## What I build

- **Workflow automation** that takes a repetitive process off your team's plate
- **Data extraction** from PDFs, emails, forms and statements into the systems you already use
- **AI assistants and agents** with a person in the loop where it matters
- **AI features** inside a web application, built on [Laravel](/services/laravel)

## How it works

We pick one process and map it properly. I build it into your existing systems, test it against your real data, and add the logging and review steps that let you trust it. You get the build, the prompts and the documentation. If it pays for itself, we look at the next one.

## Sensible, not hyped

AI is worth adding where the numbers work and nowhere else. If a simpler automation or a better form would do the job, that is what I will recommend. The articles on [AI costs and implementation](/article) go into the detail.
