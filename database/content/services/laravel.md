Most web applications are built once and then patched for years. Each patch makes the next one harder, until a small change takes a week and nobody wants to touch the code. I build Laravel applications the other way round, so they stay fast and easy to change.

## What I build

- **Web applications** shaped around how your business actually works
- **Admin panels and dashboards** your team will want to use
- **APIs and integrations** with payments, CRMs, email and anything else with an API
- **Content management** built for your content, not the other way round
- **Rebuilds and upgrades** of slow, fragile or outdated applications

## How I work

Every project starts with an audit of what is there: the code, the hosting, and a [free site audit](/tools/site-audit) of the public side. From that comes a written scope and, wherever possible, a fixed price. The build happens in small releases you can try as they land, every one covered by automated tests. At the end you get the code, the documentation and the accounts. Nothing is locked to me.

## The proof

The CMS this site runs on is mine. [cg-cms](/work/cg-cms) is a Laravel package that renders pages once on save and serves them from disk, so a cached page takes 4.4 milliseconds and the site runs comfortably on a small server. Moving [this site](/work/chrisgarlick-com) onto it kept every one of its 79 URLs working.
