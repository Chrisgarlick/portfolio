A website should load quickly, be editable without a developer, and not cost a subscription to keep online. That's achievable. It's also surprisingly rare.

## What you get

- **Design and build** around your content and what the site is for
- **A CMS you own** rather than rent: [WordPress](/services/wordpress), or [cg-cms](/work/cg-cms) on Laravel when you want something leaner
- **Speed** measured with Core Web Vitals, so it holds up on real phones
- **SEO and accessibility** done properly from the start, not bolted on later

## Who it suits

It suits a business that wants a site it can update itself, without calling a developer for every change. It also suits anyone tired of paying a platform to keep their site online.

If what you need is closer to an application than a website, [Laravel development](/services/laravel) is the better route.

## WordPress or cg-cms?

WordPress is a good choice when you want a familiar editor that many developers know. cg-cms is leaner. It renders each page once, when you save it, and serves it straight from disk. That keeps pages fast, even on a small server. I'll recommend whichever suits the site, not whichever I'd rather build.

## How it works

It starts with what the site is for and the content it needs to carry. If you have a site already, I audit it first, so we know what to keep and what to fix. Then I write a scope: the pages, the features and what done looks like.

I design and build around your content, and you can try the site as it comes together. Speed is checked with Core Web Vitals before launch. If any URLs change, redirects go in, so old links and search engines still find the right pages.

You talk to me the whole way through, and I'm the one building it. At handover you get the site, the documentation and the accounts.

## After launch

The site is yours. You own the CMS, so there's no platform fee to keep it online. I can look after updates and changes as ongoing support, or you can take it from there.

## Proof

This site is the example. [Rebuilding it](/work/chrisgarlick-com) took largest contentful paint from about 3 seconds to under 2 in lab tests, and kept every one of its 79 URLs working.

## Common questions

### Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, that's the point. Text, images and pages are edited in the CMS, without touching code. The templates are built around your content, so editing stays simple.

### Will a new site hurt my search rankings?

A planned move keeps that risk low. Every URL that changes gets a redirect, and I check the live URLs before switching over. When I rebuilt this site, all 79 URLs kept working.

### Do you use page builders?

No. Page builders add weight, and that slows pages down on real phones. I build templates around your content instead, so pages stay light.

### Do I own the site?

Yes. The CMS is yours rather than rented from a platform, so there's no monthly platform fee to keep it online. You get the code, the content and the accounts.
