A website should load quickly, be editable without a developer, and not cost a subscription to keep online. That's achievable. It's also surprisingly rare.

## What you get

- **Design and build** around your content and what the site is for
- **A CMS you own** rather than rent: [WordPress](/services/wordpress), or [cg-cms](/work/cg-cms) on Laravel when you want something leaner
- **Speed** measured with Core Web Vitals, so it holds up on real phones
- **SEO and accessibility** done properly from the start, not bolted on later

## Proof

This site is the example. [Rebuilding it](/work/chrisgarlick-com) took largest contentful paint from about 3 seconds to under 2 in lab tests, and kept every one of its 79 URLs working.
