WordPress runs a huge share of the web, and most of it could be faster, safer and easier to edit than it is.

The problem usually isn't WordPress. It's a heavy theme, a page builder and twenty plugins nobody chose on purpose. Strip that back, build what the site actually needs, and WordPress is quick, secure and pleasant to work with.

## What I do

- **Custom themes** built around your content, not a page builder
- **Custom plugins** that do exactly what the site needs and nothing more
- **Speed work** on caching, images and Core Web Vitals
- **Security** hardening, updates and sensible hosting
- **Rescues** for sites that have become slow, broken or scary to change

## Measured, not guessed

Every WordPress job starts with an audit. I built [Kritano](/work/kritano-website-audits) to check sites against more than 500 rules across SEO, accessibility, security and performance. That way the work goes where it makes a difference, and you can see the result afterwards. Try the short version with the [free site audit](/tools/site-audit).

## How a job runs

Once the audit is done, I write a scope: what changes, in what order, and what done looks like. For a rescue, that usually means fixing what's broken first, then what's slow.

You talk to me throughout, and I'm the person writing the code. At the end you get the theme, the plugins and the documentation. Nothing is locked to me, so another WordPress developer could carry on.

## Who it suits

It suits a business already on WordPress whose site has become slow, hard to edit or scary to update. It also suits a new site where a familiar editor matters.

If you'd like to keep a page builder, it's worth talking that through first. Most of the speed gains come from replacing it.

## After launch

WordPress needs looking after. Core, themes and plugins all get updates, and fewer plugins means fewer things to update. I can handle updates and security as ongoing support, or hand it to your team. Run another audit later and you can see what changed.

## When WordPress isn't the answer

Sometimes a site has outgrown it. If a custom application would serve you better, I will tell you, and [Laravel development](/services/laravel) is the other half of what I do.

## Common questions

### Can you fix a site someone else built?

Yes. Rescues are a regular part of the work. I start with an audit, so we know what's slowing the site down or breaking it before anything changes.

### Do I have to rebuild my whole site?

Not always. Sometimes targeted work on caching, images and plugins makes most of the difference. The audit shows which, and if a rebuild is the better route, I'll explain why.

### Why not use a page builder?

Page builders make the first version quick to put together. They also add a lot of weight, which slows the site down on real phones. A custom theme built around your content keeps pages light and editing simple.

### Will I still be able to edit the site?

Yes. Editing stays in the normal WordPress admin. The theme is built around your content, so it should be easier to edit than before, not harder.
