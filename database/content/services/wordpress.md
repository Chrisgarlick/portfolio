WordPress runs a huge share of the web, and most of it could be faster, safer and easier to edit than it is. The usual cause is not WordPress itself. It is a heavy theme, a page builder and a stack of plugins nobody chose on purpose.

## What I do

- **Custom themes** built around your content, with modern front-end tooling
- **Custom plugins** that do exactly what the site needs and nothing more
- **Speed work** on caching, images and Core Web Vitals
- **Security** hardening, updates and sensible hosting
- **Rescues** for sites that have become slow, broken or hard to change

## Measured, not guessed

Every WordPress job starts with an audit. I built [Kritano](/work/kritano-website-audits) to check sites against more than 500 rules across SEO, accessibility, security and performance, so the work goes where it makes a difference and you can see the result afterwards. You can try a short version with the [free site audit](/tools/site-audit).

## When WordPress is not the answer

Sometimes a site has outgrown it. If a custom application would serve you better, I will say so, and [Laravel development](/services/laravel) is the other half of what I do.
