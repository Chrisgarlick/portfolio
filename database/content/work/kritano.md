## Why I built it

Auditing a website properly meant juggling half a dozen tools: one for SEO, another for accessibility, another for security headers, another for Core Web Vitals. Each had its own report, its own scoring and its own blind spots. Kritano puts all of it into one audit, with one prioritised list of what to fix.

## What it checks

A single audit runs more than 500 rules across six areas:

- **SEO:** metadata, headings, links, crawlability
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 checks
- **Security:** headers and common misconfigurations
- **Performance:** Core Web Vitals
- **Content quality:** readability, E-E-A-T signals and answer engine optimisation
- **Structured data:** what is there, and whether it is valid

Findings are ranked by impact and come with guidance on the fix, so a report is a to-do list rather than a wall of warnings. Reports export to PDF and CSV, and scheduled audits track a site's score over time.
